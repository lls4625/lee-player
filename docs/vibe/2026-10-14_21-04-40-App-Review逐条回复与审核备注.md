# 雷player App Review 逐条回复与审核备注

## 使用前提

本文用于回复 Apple 2026 年 10 月 8 日提出的 `Guideline 2.1 - Information Needed - New App Submission`。

准备重新提交的信息：

- App 版本：`3.0.0`
- 构建版本：`17`
- Bundle ID：`vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer`
- 录制设备：实体 `iPhone 14 Pro Max`
- 系统版本：`iOS 27.0.1`
- 界面语言：简体中文
- 建议的新录屏文件名：`LeiPlayer-App-Review-Demo-3.0.0-17.mp4`

> **提交前必须完成：** 当前已有演示视频录制自构建 16，不能把下文中“录屏来自构建 17”的表述用于现有视频。请先在实体 iPhone 14 Pro Max 上安装并打开构建 17，重新录制相同流程，再将新视频作为审核回复附件上传。新视频应从主屏幕点击雷player图标开始，并包含导入文件、播放控制、画中画、打赏商品及 Apple 购买流程。

## 一、用于“回复 App 审核”的英文全文

以下内容可在构建 17 的新录屏完成并附加后，直接粘贴到“回复 App 审核”。

```text
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your feedback. We have provided the requested information below and added the same information to the App Review Information Notes field.

The attached screen recording was captured on a physical iPhone 14 Pro Max running iOS 27.0.1. It demonstrates LeiPlayer version 3.0.0, build 17. The recording begins on the iPhone Home Screen, launches the app, and demonstrates the typical user flow.

1. Screen recording and applicable flows

The attached recording demonstrates:
- Launching LeiPlayer from the iPhone Home Screen
- Importing local media through the iOS Files interface
- Opening and playing imported media
- Seeking, playback-speed adjustment, repeat modes, A-B repeat, timed stop, and other playback controls
- Picture in Picture and returning to the app
- Opening the developer-support page, viewing the submitted In-App Purchase products, and completing the Apple purchase flow

LeiPlayer does not provide account registration or login, so there is no account-deletion flow. The app does not host, publish, or share user-generated content, so content-reporting and user-blocking mechanisms are not applicable. The recording uses the Simplified Chinese interface. The same functionality is available in Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese.

2. App purpose, target audience, problem, and value

LeiPlayer is a local audio and video player designed primarily for students, language learners, course learners, and users who organize and play media files that they already own or are authorized to use.

It solves the problem of managing local course folders and repeatedly studying audio or video without requiring an account, cloud upload, streaming service, advertising, or developer-operated media server. Its value includes private on-device storage, playback-progress tracking, history, favorites, speed control, repeat playback, A-B repeat, timed stop, background audio, Picture in Picture, and external SRT/VTT subtitle support.

3. Setup and access instructions

No account, login credentials, subscription, organization membership, or special configuration is required.

To access the main features:
1) Launch LeiPlayer.
2) On the Library page, open the menu in the upper-right corner.
3) Choose the file or folder import option and select a local audio or video file using the iOS Files interface.
4) Open the imported folder and tap a media file to start playback.
5) Use the controls on the player page for seeking, speed, repeat modes, A-B repeat, subtitles, timed stop, Picture in Picture, and other playback options.

No proprietary sample file is required. Reviewers may use any short standard MP4, MOV, M4V, MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, or AIFF file available in the Files app. The attached recording demonstrates the setup and playback process with ordinary local MP4 files.

4. External services, tools, and platforms

LeiPlayer uses:
- Apple StoreKit and the App Store for In-App Purchase product information, payment processing, and transaction verification
- The iOS Files interface and Finder file sharing for user-initiated local file import
- Apple AVFoundation and AVKit for system media playback, audio sessions, and Picture in Picture
- Flutter for the app interface
- Bundled media_kit, libmpv, and FFmpeg components for local playback of additional media formats

The app does not use an external data provider, account-authentication service, cloud-storage service, streaming-content provider, advertising network, third-party analytics service, AI service, or developer-operated backend for its core functionality. Imported files and user data remain on the device.

5. Regional differences

LeiPlayer's core features and content function consistently in all regions where the app is available. The interface supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese. In-App Purchase display names, prices, currency, tax, and availability may be localized by the App Store storefront, but purchases do not change or unlock any app feature or content.

6. Regulated industry and protected third-party material

LeiPlayer does not operate in a highly regulated industry. The app does not provide, bundle, host, search, stream, download, or distribute protected third-party courses, movies, music, or other media. Users import files that they created, are authorized to use, or otherwise have the legal right to use. Therefore, no regulated-industry credential or third-party content authorization is applicable. Open-source license and attribution information for bundled software components is available inside the app.

7. In-App Purchases and navigation

LeiPlayer includes six submitted consumable In-App Purchase products with the following identifiers:
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.small
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.medium
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.large
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.xlarge
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.premium
- vip.ichiki.javalee.leeplayer.tip.strong

All six products are optional, repeatable, consumable tips that allow users to support the developer. They do not unlock any feature, content, service, or entitlement; they do not affect free use of the app; and they are not restorable.

To reach the purchase flow, tap the hand-and-heart support icon in the upper-right corner of the Library page, or open Settings and select “Support the Developer.” Select a tip product, confirm the choice, and the standard Apple In-App Purchase sheet appears.

Please let us know if any additional information is required. Thank you for reviewing LeiPlayer.
```

2) 在课程库页面打开右上角菜单；

6. 监管行业和受保护的第三方材料

雷player 不属于高度监管行业。App 不提供、内置、托管、搜索、在线播放、下载或分发受保护的第三方课程、影视、音乐或其他媒体。用户只能导入自行创作、已获授权或依法有权使用的文件，因此没有适用的行业资质或第三方内容授权文件。随包开源组件的许可和署名信息可在 App 内查看。

## 六、分析与变更记录

### 修改前情况

- 已有 Apple 原始审核信息、中文翻译和缺口核对。
- 已有构建 16 的完整演示视频，但准备重新提交的是构建 17。
- 当前 App Store Connect 已显示 `3.0.0 (17)` 上传完成并处于测试状态。
- 原审核备注只覆盖了无账号、本地媒体导入、基础播放步骤和打赏说明，没有完整逐项回应 Apple 的 1–7 项要求。

### 修改后情况

- 新增本文档，提供可直接粘贴的完整英文逐条回复、中文对照及适合备注字段的精简英文。
- 明确记录设备、系统、版本、构建号、外部服务、地区一致性、第三方内容边界和六个 IAP 的性质及入口。
- 增加构建 17 新录屏的强制前置条件和重新提交检查清单。

### 修改内容

- 仅新增 Markdown 审核回复文档。
- 未修改应用源码、配置、视频或 App Store Connect 页面。
- 未发送审核回复，未上传附件，未重新提交审核。

### 修改原因

- Apple 明确要求提供全部 1–7 项信息，并要求同样内容同时出现在审核回复和 App 审核备注中。
- 逐项编号可降低审核人员遗漏或认为答复不完整的风险。
- 录屏构建与待审构建保持一致，可避免因构建版本不符再次要求补充信息。

### 可能影响或注意事项

- 文档中关于“附加的录屏来自构建 17”的句子，只能在构建 17 新录屏实际完成并上传后使用。
- 如果 App Store Connect 备注字段提示长度超限，应优先保留编号 1–7、设备/系统/构建信息、无账号、无 UGC、外部服务、地区一致性和 IAP 说明，压缩功能枚举，不得删除整项回答。
- Apple 最终是否接受仍取决于构建 17 的实际运行情况、录屏内容和服务端审核结果。
